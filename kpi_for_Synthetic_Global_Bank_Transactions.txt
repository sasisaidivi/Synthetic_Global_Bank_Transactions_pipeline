1.Total Transaction Amount Measures the total value of all transactions.
	Shows the total monetary value of all transactions processed during a selected period. It helps measure the overall financial activity and business volume.
	
2.Total Transaction Count Measures transaction volume and overall system a2.ctivity.
	Shows the total number of transactions completed during a selected period. It helps identify transaction volume, customer activity, and system usage.
	
3.Total Deposits vs Total Credit Compares customer deposits with credit amounts to understand financial position.
	Compares the total amount deposited by customers with the total credit amount provided to them. It helps evaluate the bank’s deposit base, lending activity, and financial position.
	
4.Customer Growth Rate Measures the percentage increase in registered customers over time.
	Measures the percentage increase in registered customers over a specific period. It helps evaluate customer acquisition and business growth.
	
	Customer Growth Rate =	((Current Customers - Previous Customers) / Previous Customers) × 100
	
5.Active Subscription Rate Measures the percentage of subscriptions that are currently active.
	Shows the percentage of subscriptions that are currently active compared with the total number of subscriptions. It helps measure customer retention and subscription performance.
	
	Active Subscription Rate = (Active Subscriptions / Total Subscriptions) × 100
